Prediction markets don't fail at pricing.
They fail at resolution.

When a market's wording meets reality, someone has to read a source and decide what it means — a judgement no deterministic chain can make. Today that gap is filled by token-weighted human voting, which is slow, capturable, and has produced some famously contested settlements. Settled puts the reading itself on-chain. Each GenLayer validator independently fetches the source, forms its own verdict, and the network only settles if they agree on the outcome.

Why this needs GenLayer specifically. The consensus check here is not a hash comparison — it's five validators each running a language model over a live web page and having to arrive at the same answer. Agreement is decided programmatically on a three-value enum, so validators must match on YES / NO / UNRESOLVED while remaining free to word their reasoning differently. If they can't agree, the transaction goes undetermined and the market stays open — for an oracle, no answer beats a wrong answer.
