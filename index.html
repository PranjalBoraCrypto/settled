When a market's wording finally meets reality, someone has to read a source and rule on what it means — a judgement no deterministic chain can make. That work is handed today to committees and token-weighted votes. Settled hands it to the network itself: every validator fetches the source, reads it, and rules independently. Nothing is recorded unless they agree. A verdict nobody spends is never really tested, because being wrong costs nothing. So these findings are bound to a second contract that holds real stakes and pays them out on the result. You can watch a wallet balance change because validators read a web page. That is the claim, and everything below is the apparatus for making it survive contact with someone trying to break it. THE VERDICT MOVES MONEY Findings are spendable only at FINAL, and the payout contract reads them only at chain-finalized state — so a result waits both for the arguing to stop and for the chain to stop. THE SOURCE IS ATTESTED FIRST Before betting opens, validators fetch the source and must agree on its hash exactly. That records what it said beforehand, and it rejects sources they cannot read identically — while the pool is still empty rather than after the money is in. DEADLOCK IS A PERMITTED OUTCOME If the validators cannot agree, the transaction is undetermined, nothing is written, and the question stays open. A blank beats a wrong entry in a permanent record.
